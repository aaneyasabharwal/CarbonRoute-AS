## 7. Threats to validity

- Duration of the benchmark input: 1.00 days; diurnal, weekly and seasonal patterns are not represented.
- Dependence: workloads share snapshots (handled by the cluster bootstrap) and neighbouring snapshots share hours (not handled; intervals may be optimistic).
- Selection: only windows already observed are scored; later windows are under-represented until more data is collected.
- Ground truth: realized values include provider estimates (see the measured share).
- Workloads are synthetic (uniform mix); deadline risk uses a declared, uncalibrated runtime CV; energy is assumed.
